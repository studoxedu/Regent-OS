What makes it different

Most school software bolts on permissions. Regent OS is built on them.

A school is a hierarchy — a proprietor, principals, heads of department, bursars, teachers — and each level should see and do exactly what its role allows, and nothing more. Regent OS is built around that structure from the ground up.

Every action — approving a result, editing a fee, admitting a student — flows through a chain of authority that mirrors how the institution actually runs. Nothing happens off the record, and nothing happens above someone's station.

Questions, answered

School management software, without the guesswork.

Does Regent OS work without internet?

Yes. Regent OS is offline-tolerant by design. Staff keep working through power cuts and network outages, and the system syncs when the connection returns. No work is lost.

Can it process our results and report cards?

Yes — continuous assessment, exams, weighted grading, and printable report cards are built in, with approval steps so results cannot be altered without oversight.

How much does school management software cost in Nigeria?

Pricing depends on school size and modules. Regent OS is priced for Nigerian institutions, not converted from dollars. Contact us at rysantechnologies@gmail.com for a quote for your school.

Can one proprietor oversee multiple branches?

Yes. The governance architecture was built for exactly this — full visibility across campuses, with each branch administering only its own.
