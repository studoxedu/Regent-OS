The company

A holding company for infrastructure that institutions depend on, not just use.

Most software for schools is a feature bolted onto a spreadsheet. We build the layer underneath — attendance, governance, records, safety — the parts that have to work every single day, for administrators who can't afford for them not to.

Rysan stays in the background. What you meet is the product: named, sharp, and built for one job. The company exists to make more of them, and to make each one credible enough to stake an institution on.

Built for the day-to-day

Infrastructure earns trust by being boring and reliable. We optimize for the thousandth morning, not the demo.

Governance at the core

Every product is built on a real permission and oversight model — who can see, change, and approve what — not access bolted on later.

Made for this market

Priced, powered, and designed for Nigerian institutions — offline-tolerant, SMS-first where it counts, no assumptions about bandwidth.

Herald
