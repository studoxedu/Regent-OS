Frequently asked

How does RFID school attendance work?

Each student carries a small RFID tag — often a sticker on the backpack. A long-range reader at the school gate detects the tag as the student passes, logging arrival and departure automatically, with no tapping or stopping.

Is long-range RFID better than tap cards or fingerprint scanners?

For a busy gate, usually yes. Long-range RFID needs no queue, no physical contact, and no action from the child, so a whole class can be logged in seconds — unlike tap cards or biometric scanners that process one student at a time.
