Frequently asked

What is the main difference between Regent OS and SAFSMS?

The clearest difference is the permission model. Regent OS is built around a governance architecture — who can see, change, and approve what, at every level — rather than treating roles as an add-on. The full article compares both systems feature by feature.

Does Regent OS work offline like a locally installed system?

Yes. Regent OS is offline-tolerant: staff keep working through power cuts and network outages, and the system syncs when the connection returns, while still being available on desktop and web.
